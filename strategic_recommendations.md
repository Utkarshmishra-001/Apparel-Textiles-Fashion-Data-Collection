# Week 4: Evaluation, Reporting and Strategic Recommendations

## Objective

Evaluate the analysis and visualization work completed during Weeks 1-3
and develop practical strategic recommendations for the Apparel,
Textiles & Fashion sector.

## Evaluation Areas

- Data analysis effectiveness
- Visualization effectiveness
- Data quality
- Business relevance
- Limitations
- Future opportunities

## Key Areas Evaluated

- Product assortment
- Pricing
- Discounts
- Materials
- Colours
- Customer ratings
- Inventory planning

## Strategic Recommendations

1. Use category-level analysis for assortment planning.
2. Develop category-specific pricing strategies.
3. Evaluate discounts using sales and profitability data.
4. Combine material analysis with sourcing and quality information.
5. Use historical data for seasonal colour planning.
6. Analyze customer reviews along with ratings.
7. Integrate inventory and sales data for demand planning.
8. Develop interactive dashboards using Power BI.

## Future Scope

- Larger datasets
- Historical data
- Sales data
- Inventory data
- Customer reviews
- Sentiment analysis
- Power BI dashboards
- Predictive analytics
- Demand forecasting

## Conclusion

Week 4 converts the analytical findings from the previous weeks into
practical business recommendations and identifies opportunities for
future data-driven decision making.
