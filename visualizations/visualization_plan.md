# Week 3 Data Visualization Plan

## Objective

Design visualizations that communicate insights from apparel, textiles and fashion data clearly and effectively.

## KPIs

- Total Products
- Average Sale Price
- Average Discount
- Average Customer Rating
- Most Common Category
- Most Common Material
- Price Range

## Planned Visualizations

### 1. Product Category Mix
Chart: Horizontal Bar Chart

Purpose:
Identify the most represented apparel categories.

### 2. Average Sale Price by Category
Chart: Column Chart

Purpose:
Compare price positioning across product categories.

### 3. Price Distribution
Chart: Histogram

Purpose:
Understand the distribution of apparel product prices.

### 4. Discount Distribution
Chart: Histogram

Purpose:
Understand promotional discount patterns.

### 5. Category Price Variation
Chart: Box Plot

Purpose:
Identify price variation and potential outliers.

### 6. Material Mix
Chart: Bar Chart

Purpose:
Identify commonly used materials.

### 7. Colour Mix
Chart: Bar Chart

Purpose:
Identify frequently occurring product colours.

### 8. Discount vs Rating
Chart: Scatter Plot

Purpose:
Investigate a possible relationship between discounts and customer ratings.

### 9. Time-Based Trend
Chart: Line Chart

Purpose:
Track changes in price, discount or category share over time when historical data is available.

## Dashboard Layout

Top:
- Total Products
- Average Sale Price
- Average Discount
- Average Rating

Middle:
- Category Mix
- Average Price by Category
- Price Distribution
- Discount Distribution

Bottom:
- Material Mix
- Colour Mix
- Discount vs Rating
- Time Trend

## Design Guidelines

- Use clear chart titles.
- Label all axes.
- Display appropriate units.
- Avoid unnecessary 3D effects.
- Use consistent category names.
- Avoid excessive colours.
- Use annotations for important findings.
- Use readable fonts and sufficient contrast.
